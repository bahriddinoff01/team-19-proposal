About the project

From an idea to a full trip, in minutes

AI Travel Planner is a web platform that turns a traveler's destination, budget, and interests into a ready-to-use, day-by-day itinerary. Users can save their plans and come back to them anytime.

Planning a trip usually takes hours of searching across blogs, maps, and booking sites. Our goal was to let AI do that work in under a minute, while keeping every plan personal.
