Overview

The problem and our solution

⏳

The problem

  • Planning a trip takes hours across blogs, maps, and booking sites.
  • Generic itineraries ignore a traveler's budget and interests.
  • Plans end up scattered in notes, screenshots, and tabs.
✨

Our solution

  • An AI planner that builds a day-by-day itinerary in under a minute.
  • Plans shaped by destination, trip length, budget, and interests.
  • A personal dashboard to save trips and favorites.
02
Core features

What the platform does

🧭

AI planning

Personalized itineraries based on interests, time, and budget.

💰

Smart budgeting

An estimated budget shown before planning, so users travel with confidence.

🎯

Personalization

Food, nature, culture, shopping, sports: users choose what they love.

🔖

Saved trips

User accounts keep favorite plans ready to reopen anytime.

Plus: a destination slider, a "Surprise Me" option for undecided travelers, light and dark mode, and a fully responsive layout.
